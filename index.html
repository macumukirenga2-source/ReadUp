About ReadUp

ReadUp ni urubuga rugamije gufasha abantu gusoma, kwandika no gusangiza abandi ibihangano, ibicuruzwa na serivisi.

Privacy Policy

ReadUp ikoresha amakuru utanga igihe ufungura konti cyangwa wohereza ubusabe. Amakuru akoreshwa mu gucunga konti no kurinda urubuga. Email yawe ntigaragazwa ku mugaragaro. Ibikorwa byemejwe bishobora kugaragara ku rubuga.

Amabwiriza yo gukoresha ReadUp

  • Shyiraho ibintu ufitiye uburenganzira bwo gukoresha.
  • Ntushyireho ibintu bitemewe n'amategeko.
  • Ubusabe bwo gutangaza bugomba kubanza kwemezwa.
  • ReadUp ishobora gukuraho ibinyuranyije n'amabwiriza.

Contact

Ku bibazo cyangwa ubufasha, andikira ubuyobozi bwa ReadUp kuri email yawe y'ubuyobozi.
